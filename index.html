Good questions.

Are lab grown diamonds real?

Yes. They have the same crystal structure and optical properties as mined diamonds, and are graded by the same labs.

Will they come with a certificate?

Every stone above 0.30 ct ships with an IGI or SGL certificate, and the report number is laser inscribed on the girdle.

What about resale value?

Lab grown prices keep falling as technology improves, so buy for love and wear, not as an investment.

How long does a custom piece take?

Ready designs ship in about a week. Custom pieces usually take two to three weeks.
