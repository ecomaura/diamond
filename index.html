Good to know

Is a lab-grown diamond a real diamond?

Yes. It has the same chemical makeup, hardness and brilliance as a mined diamond. Only the origin differs.

Will it come with a certificate?

Every stone is graded by IGI or GIA and laser-inscribed with its report number.

Which grades do you recommend?

Cut matters most. E to G colour and VS clarity look flawless to the eye and keep the price sensible. We will advise on your budget.

Can you make a custom design?

Yes. Send a sketch or a photo on WhatsApp and we will quote the stone and the setting.
