A lab-grown diamond is carbon crystallised in the same structure as a mined one. It is grown in weeks instead of billions of years, with a smaller footprint and a traceable source.

Choosing a stone

Four qualities decide how a diamond looks and what it costs. We will tell you where paying more shows, and where it does not.

Cut

Decides the sparkle. Spend here first. Ask for Excellent or Ideal.

Colour

D to F is icy white. G to H still faces up white once set.

Clarity

VS1 and VS2 are clean to the naked eye. Higher grades rarely show.

Carat

Weight, not size. Shape and cut change how large a stone appears.
