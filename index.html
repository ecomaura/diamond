The four Cs, in plain words

Cut

Decides sparkle. Always choose Excellent or Ideal. It matters more than any other C.

Colour

D to F looks icy white. G to H is nearly the same to the eye and saves money.

Clarity

VS1 and above is clean to the naked eye. Don’t pay extra for flawless.

Carat

Weight, not size. Lab-grown lets you go up a size without moving your budget.

Questions we hear often

Are lab-grown diamonds real?

Yes. They have the same crystal structure and hardness as mined diamonds, and they’re graded by the same labs.

Will it come with a certificate?

Every stone above 0.30 ct comes with an IGI or GIA report, plus a laser inscription on the girdle.

Can I return or upgrade later?

You can exchange within 30 days, and upgrade against your original value any time after that.

Do you ship outside Gujarat?

We ship insured across India and to most countries. Share your pin code or country on WhatsApp for timing.
