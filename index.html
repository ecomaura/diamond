Why Lab-Grown Diamonds

The same diamond. A modern point of view.

  • 01

    Identical in nature

    Lab-grown diamonds share the same fundamental physical and chemical properties as mined diamonds: pure carbon in a diamond crystal lattice.

  • 02

    Brilliance and fire

    Cut to exacting proportions, they return light with the same optical character — scintillation, sparkle and spectral fire.

  • 03

    Advanced technology

    Grown in controlled environments by sophisticated processes, then cut and polished by specialists.

  • 04

    More accessible luxury

    Greater size, quality and design freedom for your investment.

  • 05

    Conscious, modern luxury

    A considered choice for those who value transparency and a contemporary approach to fine jewellery.
