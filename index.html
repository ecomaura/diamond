Every Facet. Every Detail. Extraordinary.

We choose each diamond under daylight-balanced light, judging how it handles light before we look at the paperwork. Cutters, setters and polishers work within metres of each other, so every detail is checked by the hands that made it.

Facets are aligned, prongs are tested and every setting is polished until the metal disappears and the diamond remains.

Why lab-grown

The same diamond. A newer story.

A short guide to what matters when you choose.

i

Identical in nature

Lab-grown diamonds share the same fundamental physical and chemical properties as mined diamonds, and are graded on the same scales.

ii

Brilliance and fire

Light behaves the same way in both. What decides sparkle is the cut, which is why we choose proportion before size.

iii

Grown with technology

Diamonds are grown in controlled conditions, which lets us select exact colour and clarity for each piece.

iv

More accessible luxury

A larger or finer stone can fit the same budget, so you can choose the diamond you actually want.

v

Modern, considered luxury

A fully disclosed origin and a stone that suits how you live now.
